Safety Notes

This build is tuned for clear trust signals, low wallet risk, and predictable on-chain behavior.

🛡️ Trust Layer
🔐
Score Storage Only

The smart contract is designed for score tracking and should not custody user funds.

🚫
No Approvals

The frontend does not ask for token approvals, permit signatures, or asset transfer permissions.

🧭

How It Works

Quick usage notes for the full on-chain flow.

  1. Play locally and keep moving as long as you want.
  2. Connect your wallet only when you are ready to submit a valid final score.
  3. Switch to Base Mainnet if prompted by your wallet.
  4. You can submit your current score at any point in a run, even before game over.
  5. Refresh the leaderboard after confirmation to see the updated ranking.
